# KitaFlux Brand Assets

**Official name:** KitaFlux  
**Tagline:** Global income. Clear local numbers.

KitaFlux is a modern financial workspace for Filipino freelancers who earn internationally and need accurate local PHP records for invoicing, payments, foreign exchange, fees, income history, and tax-ready reporting.

## What is current
- `colors.json`, `brand.json` and `brand-guidelines.md`: the palette, fonts and rules, derived from the gradient K mark. `src/app/globals.css` is built from them.
- The mark itself lives in `public/brand/` (`web-logo.png`, `social-avatar.png`, `og-image.png`, `email-header.png`).

## Retired
`logo/*.svg`, `favicon/*` and `examples/usage.md` belong to the earlier geometric mark. They are kept for history only and are not referenced by the app. Do not use them.
